SCOPE NOTE

The Control interface (platform 05) is deliberately unfinished by design, not by accident: random, greedy and wfm_heuristic are hand-written rules that exist only to make the simulation runnable end to end. No learning is wired in — rl/action, rl/observation and rl/reward sit empty, and stable-baselines3 is commented out of requirements.txt — because the project's stated priority is getting the generative world (platforms 01–04) right before anything is asked to learn from it.
